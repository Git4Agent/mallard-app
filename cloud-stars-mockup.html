Where I would land

H1 with C2. The split pill is the shape people already know, and in the desktop's box grammar it looks native rather than borrowed; its fixed count cell keeps the head still when the number changes. On the card, the footer is a phrase read left to right, and C1 cuts its first word on exactly the cards that have the most going on; C2 keeps that phrase whole and pays with name width only on cards that carry stars, where the name was already ellipsized. A zero stays silent in both, so today's listing does not change. Take H3 if you want the head quieter, and C3 only if starring from the listing matters more than keeping one target per card.
