Mallard Cloud in the pelican's idiom

Mallard already has most of that artifact's grammar: rounded boxes with a hard shadow, pill controls, a rounded display face. What it lacks is four things, and the three directions below differ only by how many of them they take. Every colour is an existing token or a mix of two; the rows are staging's own, in the listing's order.

A — The readout

A mono eyebrow, a heavy title, and a readout of big tabular figures, with honey as the one filled accent. No picture. Ships as CSS and a few lines of JSX; the rows are unchanged.

Where I would land

B. The picture is what makes the artifact memorable, and drawing it on the pixel grid keeps it in the same hand as the duck logo and every glyph in the rows, so nothing else on the site has to change. A is B without the plate, which is also what B should collapse to on a phone.

C is the nearest to the artifact, and the cost is real: ink borders and ink shadows on twelve stacked rows are heavy, and a smooth, outlined duck next to a pixel logo and pixel glyphs reads as two artists. Doing C properly means redrawing the icon set too.

Two things to decide either way. The readout gives page-wide totals, not the per-row counts you removed, but the listing is cursor-paged, so figures past the first page need a count from the service. And the eyebrow is decoration: drop it and the rest still stands.
