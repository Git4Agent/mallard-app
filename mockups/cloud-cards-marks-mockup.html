Four ways to seat the marks

All four keep what is now on staging: the glyph in a 26 px gutter, the name, facts, and footer on one left edge, fixed slots so figures sit under their neighbours', no type words. They differ in where the provider marks sit and in what an empty slot shows. The empty-slot gap is the thing that still reads as misaligned on staging: a dash in the slot says "none" the way a table does, instead of leaving a hole.

F1 Marks lead the ledger staging today, with dashes

Marks are the first ledger column, then sessions, files, size. Empty slots show a dim dash, so Mossy's line reads "◇ · – · 1 · 23.6 KiB" and the columns never look like holes.

  • One change from today. Only the dash is new.
  • Marks near the name. First thing after the glyph gutter.
  • Marks column is fixed width. A third provider would need a wider slot.

F2 Marks on the name line before the badge

The marks move up beside the name, ahead of the badge, like GitHub's language dot beside a repository name in search results. The ledger then starts at the left edge with sessions, and the name line carries the two things that identify the repository: what made it, and who can see it.

  • Ledger starts flush. Sessions is the first column on every card.
  • Identity on one line. Name, provider, visibility.
  • Name loses ~36px. Sixteen-character names still fit at four across; longer ones cut.

F3 Right rail badge, marks, date stacked at the right

The card gets a second alignment edge: the badge, the marks, and the date each end at the right on their own line. Left edge for what a thing is, right edge for its attributes. The ledger keeps the left with sessions first.

  • Two clean edges. Everything on the right is an attribute; everything on the left is content.
  • Name keeps its width. Only the badge shares its line.
  • Marks drift from the name. They read as a property of the card, not the title.

F4 Two lines the footer folds away

The pusher's avatar joins the name line at the right (GitHub search results show the owner's avatar there), the date is dropped from the card and left to the sort order, and the marks end the ledger row. Two lines, the shortest card that still aligns.

  • Shortest. ~80px per card; four rows before scrolling.
  • Still a ledger. Same slots, same edges.
  • No date on the card. "Newest" sort carries recency; the repository page shows the date.
  • Avatar without a name. The login lives in the tooltip.

Where I would land

F3. Two alignment edges is what makes a grid of small cards read as calm: content on the left, attributes on the right, the same on every card. The dash in an empty slot belongs to all four and is the fix for the hole you still see on staging. F2 is the choice if the marks should read as part of the name; F4 if you want the grid as short as it can be.
